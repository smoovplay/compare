# SmoovPlay comparison clips

The page at https://smoovplay.github.io/compare/ plays six clips of a 30 fps PS5 Remote Play stream against
[SmoovPlay](https://github.com/smoovplay/smoovplay)'s output at its real frame rate (60 or 90 fps): split down the
middle, side by side, or one at a time, flipping between the two on the same moment. It also has every frame of the
90 fps mode slowed 3×. Reddit and most social sites play video at 60 fps or less.

The clips use Rockstar Games' published GTA VI gameplay (An Extended Look), run through SmoovPlay as a simulated
1080p30 Remote Play stream. Demonstration only; nothing here is monetized. Grand Theft Auto VI © Rockstar Games.
