Works on any screen. This is the 90 fps mode played at a third of the speed: each real frame from the PS5 is followed by two drawn by the AI.

SmoovPlay plays your console on your PC and uses the graphics card to draw extra frames, so a 30 fps game runs at 60, 90 or up to 120 fps to match your screen.

Free and open source for Windows 10 and 11: github.com/smoovplay/smoovplay
